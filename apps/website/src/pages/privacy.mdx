# Privacy Policy

This policy describes the **Lumen Read browser extension**. Reading works locally:
books, annotations, chat history and reading progress are stored in your browser.
The published extension does not enable analytics or Sentry error reporting.

## Optional AI

Cloud AI is optional. If you choose a provider and grant access and content-sharing
consent, your API key is sent to that provider for authentication. Chat messages,
conversation context and relevant book excerpts are sent to generate answers.
Annotations are included only when you explicitly enable their sharing. These
requests go directly to your selected provider, not to a Lumen-operated server.
The provider's own retention and privacy policies apply.

API keys are kept for the current session, not persisted with reader settings.
On-device inference does not upload your books. Optional model downloads contact
the model host, which can receive the usual network information such as your IP
address. A loopback provider sends requests to the local server you configure.

## Optional Dropbox sync

If you authorize Dropbox, library records, annotations, saved chat sessions, reading positions
and typography are synchronized directly with Dropbox, without passing through
our servers. Dropbox credentials and refresh tokens are used for authentication;
the refresh token is stored locally so your authorized connection can persist.
Dropbox's own privacy policies apply.

## Your choices

You can keep reading without enabling either feature. Cloud content sharing is
controlled in AI settings; Dropbox can be disconnected in synchronization settings.
In Firefox you can also grant or revoke optional data permissions in the
extension's **Permissions and data** panel. Revocation blocks future requests; it
cannot retrieve information already sent to a service. Disconnecting a service
does not automatically delete data that service previously received.

UI fonts and icons are bundled locally and do not require Google Fonts requests.
